Stay awake on the road

Mount your phone where its camera can see your face. If your eyes close for 1 second, a loud alarm buzzes for 3 seconds and keeps going until they open.

Camera video never leaves this device

Active

Normal blinking is ignored. Eyes open, you're good.

Drowsy

Heavy eyelids, long blinks or repeated yawns: a soft warning chime.

Sleeping

Eyes closed too long: loud 3-second buzz, vibration and a flashing screen.

Night light

Too dark to see your face? The screen turns white to light it up.

Before you drive

  1. Mount the phone on the dashboard, front camera facing you, about an arm's length away.
  2. Turn the media volume up. On iPhone, check the Silent switch too.
  3. Plug in a charger. The camera and a bright screen use a lot of battery.
  4. Keep this app on screen. On Android you can split the screen with your maps app.

This is a safety aid, not a cure for tiredness. If you feel sleepy, stop somewhere safe and rest: a 20-minute nap helps.

Face tracking runs on your device with MediaPipe (Apache-2.0).
